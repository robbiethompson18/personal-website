EffortMaxxing Dating

Published November 15, 2026Last edited November 15, 2026

Finding a partner is very important to long-term happiness, about 10x as good as moving up one income decile, or 90% as good as being employed.⁠[1]

Yet most people feel helpless. Finding love is a lot less tractable than getting a promotion.

This post assumes you want to put in more hours in search of love, and don’t know how to spend them.

TLDR: Meeting people is hard and I don’t have a good answer. Once you meet someone:

  • Ask them out explicitly.
  • Do a lot of emotional labor.
  • Maximize information.

Ask People Out #

People in your social network. You will get rejected. Do it anyway! Aim for a 50% hit rate.

I find it best to:

  1. Get familiar with someone. Spend time with them in ways that are not romantic, and would be normal as friends. Then:
  2. Very unambiguously ask them out and get a clean yes or no.

Step 2 avoids interminable flirting, both the variety which goes nowhere despite mutual interest and the variety which is smothering and rude.

Alternative: tell someone that you have a crush on their friend. If the feeling is mutual, this info will get ‘leaked’ to both parties.

While Dating: Do Emotional Labor #

People use calendar time to dictate how serious a relationship should be. I try to crush this metric, to get emotionally close to a partner in a small amount of calendar time. Or break up quickly. Life is short and I want to be in love!

Step 1: make a lot of time for the person you’re dating.

Step 2: make the most of that time:

  • Be vulnerable. Tell the other person your biggest insecurities. Accept that if things go poorly you will be more hurt.
  • Introduce your partner to your friends. Meet theirs. Accept the awkwardness and the judgement.
  • Journal a lot about the other person, talk about them with your friends. Be in touch with your emotional state. Start hard conversations, or accept your partner’s imperfections, instead of letting issues fester subconsciously.
  • Write handwritten letters. You can spill your emotions without risk of misspeaking. Letters are easy for your partner to consume.

While Dating: InfoMaxx #

Maximize your information about your partner, and their info about you. Do things that will make you either fall in love or hate the person’s guts.

Don’t hide unflattering info. Actively bring it up early:

  • Tell your partner you hate clubbing, even if you’ll go anyway to support them.
  • Let your partner meet that friend you love despite how toxic they are.
  • (Delicately) mention your controversial political opinions.

Try very hard to communicate flattering information about yourself:

  • Take your partner backpacking to show how good you are outdoors.
  • Cook a great meal to show off your skills in the kitchen.
  • Bring up the topics you geek out about.

I Am Not Sure: Swiping #

I tried to Fermi estimate how much more efficient real-life flirting is than swiping on the apps, and the conclusion wasn’t nearly as clear as I’d hoped it would be. The only clear inefficiency is that I don’t ask enough people out.

Coda: On Serendipity #

The women I have dated even a little bit seriously I met through circumstances mostly out of my control:

  • We were interns together.
  • We met at a frat party.
  • A mutual friend invited a big group on a weekend trip.
  • We met on Hinge (which does feel in my control in the statistical sense).
  • She found my dateme doc.

To get a sense for how serendipitous the dateme doc was: Alice (pseudonym) asked Claude to find dateme docs she would be into. Claude found my doc in a place I did not post it: I suspect that someone made a registry and bootstrapped it with profiles from other places. Alice and I went on a date, and Alice showed several friends my doc to catch them up on her life. When Alice and I decided not to go on a second date, one of said friends asked me out, and that friend is the girl I am serious about.

I have no idea how to find people to date besides having a vibrant social life. I doubt whether the apps or dateme docs help very much. Though making a dateme doc is very fun, so I recommend doing it anyway. Here is mine!
