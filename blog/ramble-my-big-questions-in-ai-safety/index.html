Ramble: my big questions in AI safety

Published September 10, 2026Last edited September 10, 2026

Alignment #

Is technnical alignment as theoretically difficult as Yudkowsky claims it is?

How powerful of AI can we create while putting off the ‘big questions’? What are the big questions?

  • If we are utilitarian
  • If AI should engage in agency morality vs care morality
  • How rigorously we need to define people’s rights and who is a moral subject

Is it even desirable to live in a world where humans are not the most powerful being? People seem to really like God. These seems like a very powerful and desirable meme. Maybe AI can fill that role?

Security #

How crux-y is compute verification / technical aspects of a treaty? Are we very close to solving this, or quite far? And am I personally way too non-knowledgable to be helpful with this?

Control #

What is the end goal of the control? Do we really expect to catch models red handed, where they admit ‘ah yeah I was trying to take over the world’? Can you make a model organism for this? How do you trade off ‘it’s really trying’ with ‘holy shit this thing is hilariously dangerous’? Are these models organisms even net-good? What if we just teach Sama how to make an AI with secret allegiances that he can hide?

What updates can we ever make? If Astra showing up as aligned on benchmarks is bad, what is good?

Open Weights #

How to weight the trade-off between open-weight models providing decentralization and good research fodder, and them potentially being dangerous in the hands of terrorists?

Employment #

What the hell are we going to do with our time?

Rogue Deployments #

What do the first days of the robot war look like?

Policy #

Why is there no deal to slow down in the works? It seems like basically everyone wants one except Donny T? Yuck that really is the reason isn’t it. He did a good job with COVID but will fumble the bag on AI.
