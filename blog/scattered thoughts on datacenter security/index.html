scattered thoughts on datacenter security

★★☆☆☆

Published November 22, 2026Last edited November 22, 2026

Epistemic status: I don’t work at a lab, and I don’t have close friends who do, and I’ve never done large (>x flops) training runs, but I have spent ~200 hours of my life thinking about these topics. So take all numbers and claims in this post as the best guess of an informed amature armed with lots of AI help.

The AI Safety community is correctly freaking out about security at frontier AI labs. If we can’t make a deal with China to pause, and the US pauses unilaterally, and then China steals model weights, then we are forced to race again to not fall behind.

Here are some questions I hope to deconfuse myself about:

  • The world seems to believe that frontier labs are thoroughly infilitrated, both in cyberspace and by employees who are MSS.⁠[1] Yet the labs seem to care a lot about security.
  • There are many things the CCP could steal: weights, algorithms, RL environments, RL rollouts, source code, etc. What is important? What is likely to go?
  • How to model the CCP? Asleep at the wheel? Very wise to AGI and acting clueless so that they can steal the weights at the right moment?
  • what things are easy to steal? Easy to secure? and vice versa?

What to steal: #

Model Weights #

I speculate that frontier models are about ~10T params, or ~10TB of data. This fits onto one external hard drive you could buy on Amazon. Cursed.⁠[2] With the weights you instantly have your adversary’s best model. Even if the Anthropic trained a model to do only Anthropic’s bidding, it would be trivial for the CCP to undo this. Models are or will soon be good enough that they can improve themselves, subject to the constraings on how many experiments they can run on your compute. So if China gets GPT 10 the could be off to the races.
