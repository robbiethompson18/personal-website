My Favorite Books and Authors

Published February 5, 2025Last edited August 26, 2025

This list is not complete. I plan to update it periodically.

Authors #

  1. Scott Alexander (Astral Codex Ten, Slate Star Codex)

    1. How I feel reading Alexander is how I imagine a 13-year-old Isaac Newton would feel reading a Calc BC textbook. So many patterns that I was barely conscious of he has named, explained, and expanded, in ways that consistently change my opinions and make me laugh.

  2. Michael Lewis

    1. “I would read an 800-page history of the stapler if he wrote it.”— someone else

  3. Nassim Taleb

    1. Taleb chose to put this on the back of Skin in the Game instead of some fluff from an NYT reviewer: “The problem isn’t that Taleb is an asshole. He is an asshole. The problem is that he’s right” — attributed to Dan from the Czech Republic on Twitter⁠[1]

  4. Christopher McDougall

  5. Michael Pollan

  6. Paul Graham

    1. I have read none of his books, but the essays on his website are spectacular. I care about startups and programming less than PG does, so I tend to avoid essays on these topics.
    2. His best work, in my opinion: Cities and Ambition, Lies We Tell Kids, It’s Charisma, Stupid, The Submarine, The Top of My Todo List, How to Disagree
