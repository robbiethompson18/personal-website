Should I work for Assymetic security?

Their Theory of Change #

  1. Cybersecurity is extremely important in the AI era.
  2. They are differentially improving cyber defense.

I think this is pretty reasonable.

Critiques #

  1. Current work is pretty small peanuts: email investigations.
  2. Either you’re a:
    • a) Data company. In which case it’s not obvious that getting DFIR data to labs makes them differentially better at defense: Mythos might just get better at avoiding detection.
    • b) Ops/product company. In which case this is just medium impact? Just doing good ol’ cyber work.

Moonshots #

Things that I would be trying to pivot into from where Asymmetric is right now that could be very important:

  1. AI threat forensics. Which model committed this cyberattack? On behalf of which entity? How did they jailbreak the model? Can we hold the provider liable?
  2. Lab investigations. Be the best at reading logs. Investigate the sandbox breakout incidents.
  3. AI-in-the-wild monitoring. Constantly scrape the internet for AI that has escaped its sandbox. Does this make any sense? So much of the internet is AI. Idk what exactly you’d be looking for. I guess you could set up honeypots, but at that point you’re really a different company.

Things I Would Learn #

  1. Research mentorship from Alex
  2. Forensics mentorship / reps
  3. Engineering reps
  4. Connections in AI Safety space

Personal Reasons For and Against #

For

  1. Would be really fun to work with Pippa
  2. Would be nice to have a job again
  3. Trying remote work is probably a good idea though I doubt I’ll like it
  4. Nice exceuse to half-commit

Against

  1. Not committing to something again
  2. Remote sucks (could maybe spend some time in London)

Conclusion #

Eh this is an OK case, enough to do a work trial. I think it’s much worse than working on more critical tech with very uncertain sign:

  • SL5 datacenters
  • Compute verification for treaties
  • Lobbying the government
  • BCI

Compute verification seems above and away to be the most important thing? I should probably reach out to Daboh sooner rather than later? It’s not clear to me how far this tech is from fruition?
