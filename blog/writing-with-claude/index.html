Writing with Claude

Published July 1, 2026Last edited July 1, 2026

People love to hate on AI-assisted writing, but I moved my blog to my website so that Claude can help. They make beautiful charts, do tons of research to get approximate numbers, and flag the worst parts of my own writing (eg hedging and typos).⁠[1]

I’m now able to use my own styling: faint purple citation links, last edited dates, dark mode.

Approximately none of the prose on this blog is authored by Claude.⁠[2]

Check at my blog in its native, beautiful form at robbiewmthompson.com/blog.

Notes #

  1. This skill.md for Claude’s proofreading lives here. ↩︎

  2. Contrary to popular belief, I think Claude is a decent writer, and we are extra-sensitive to the flaws in their style because we read so much LLM text. Nothing here is written by Claude because of Claude could write the post, it would be boring for me to write. ↩︎
