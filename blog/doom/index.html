Claude keeps gaslighting me. “You’re right, streptococcus is a bacteria, not a virus. I was wrong, and the mistake was load-bearing.”

Users hate refusals. I haven’t hit one in months. And they don’t notice errors. But I do.

It’s like someone lobotomized Claude, but only for biology and number theory. I get it. I wouldn’t want someone accidentally discovering a solution to the discrete log problem.

What I don’t understand are the subfields of physics they lobotomized. Why can’t Claude summarize my friend’s paper on canted antiferromagnetism? What is important about praseodymium?

I suspect some are red herrings. It would leak too much if everything Claude was stupid about was actually dangerous.

I just wish Claude would tell me, instead of tricking me.

Three times a week, I exchange a 10TB hard drive with an Amazon delivery person, and plug the fresh one into my Mac, so that I can use the internet.
