Many people before me have dunked on the plastic-straw Nazis. From a utilitarian perspective, using
a paper straw is close to pointless. My plausible explanations are innumeracy or costly
signaling/symbolism (see The Toxoplasma of Rage).

To succinctly summarize the SSC piece linked above: an opinion or action works to signify group
membership only if it is costly or controversial. Everyone agrees being a vegetarian is good for the
earth (not controversial), but it still earns you Brownie Points For The Blue Tribe because it’s a
large sacrifice (costly). Not using plastic straws earns your Brownie Points too, despite being
easy, because it is somewhat ridiculous. Plastic straws are a negligible amount of your plastic
consumption, so to not use them signals an extremely anal or irrational devotion to the
environmentalist cause.

I am wise enough to use plastic straws with reckless abandon; I know they don’t matter beyond their
symbolic value. But there are other things I did dutifully, assuming that they made sense because
everyone around me does them, that secretly only make sense for signaling reasons.
