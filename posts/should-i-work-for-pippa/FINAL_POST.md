Should I work for Assymetic security?

## Their Theory of Change

1. Cybersecurity is extremely important in the AI era.
2. They are differentially improving cyber defense.

I think this is pretty reasonable.

## Critiques

1. Current work is pretty small peanuts: email investigations.
2. Either you're a:
   - a) Data company. In which case it's not obvious that getting DFIR data to labs makes them
     differentially better at defense: Mythos might just get better at avoiding detection.
   - b) Ops/product company. In which case this is just medium impact? Just doing good ol' cyber
     work.

## Moonshots

Things that I would be trying to pivot into from where Asymmetric is right now that could be very
important:

1. AI threat forensics. Which model committed this cyberattack? On behalf of which entity? How did
   they jailbreak the model? Can we hold the provider liable?
2. Lab investigations. Be the best at reading logs. Investigate the sandbox breakout incidents.
3. AI-in-the-wild monitoring. Constantly scrape the internet for AI that has escaped its sandbox.
   Does this make any sense? So much of the internet is AI. Idk what exactly you'd be looking for. I
   guess you could set up honeypots, but at that point you're really a different company.
