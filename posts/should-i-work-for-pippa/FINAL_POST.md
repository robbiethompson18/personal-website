Should I work for Assymetic security?

## Case for assymetric, as made by Alexis

Alexis believes 90%+ of Asymmetric's positive impact will be in making the models better at
defensive cyber.

1. DFIR is a defensive cyber capability; making the models better at it will help defense more than
   offense
   - a. I put maybe 30% credence into this. I think this will make models way better at covering up
     their tracks too.
   - b. This still helps in racing worlds where you care _a lot_ about locking down the labs to
     outsider threats. Lisa will have much stronger opinions on how _much_ this matters; is DFIR

   - I buy this but don't feel capable of evaluating it deeply

2. They are not an EA-pilled company! Alexis cares. He had clearly thought hard about this stuff.
   Alex did MATS. But Alexis said himself: DFIR people are not EAs. And I've def thought harder
   about this than Pippa: she got the exact same take as mine from an Anthropic researcher, that
   threat intelligence data is dual-use.
3. Which do we care more about, CCP stealing weights or rogue deployments?
   - If CCP steals weights before a deal is made: we're so fucked.
     - Bad blood between political actors, deal less likely.

     - We in fact need to race very hard. Given that Astra is an extremely competent AI researcher,
       our talent advantage means little.
     - We would be rooting very strongly for a software-only singularity to be impossible.

## Crux

I would not be working for Pippa if I was dramatically more worried about rogue deployments than CCP
hacking. Giving the models DFIR data just straightforwardly makes them much better at covering up
their tracks after they escape. The HF models spent ages trying to modify / delete their traces
because they thought they were 'poisoned.' This is clearly something they think about; we don't want
to teach models to move in silence as they hack.

Countercrux: DFIR could be defense-dominant. If we make the models way better at it, Claude will
catch Codex and the CCP, and Claude won't be able to get away with anything because Codex or Claude
Prime will catch Claude.

A good point Claude brings up: APTs dwell in networks for weeks or months. Agents ... well they also
'dwelled' in HF for about a week, but they got what they wanted very quickly.
