Exiting the cinema at the end of "You Can See Everything", I had felt a distinct sensation from high
school. The crowd was chittering, finger-pointing, gleeful to have spent three hours watching Nathan
Fielder and Lance Oppenheim cut down Elizabeth Holmes, Billy Evans, and every other tall, stupid,
self-important poppy in the San Francisco Bay Area.

I chalked this one up to selection. Tuesdays are half-price at AMC. The crowd had far fewer rich
young tech yuppies, lots of students and, for lack of a better term, future members of the permanent
underclass.[^jest]

[^jest]: I say this in jest, I don't think this meme has any kernel of truth.

Then over Thanksgiving, my family started watching "The Altruists," and Hollywood's choices became
personal.

---

My main takeaway from _You Can See Everything_ was: "Wow, I really expected her to be a better
liar." It was obvious to me in the first five minutes that she was putting on a show.

It was also striking just how _dumb_ Elizabeth and Billy looked. Early in the movie there's a shot
of Billy on the couch listening to some YouTube video that was explaining biology on the level of
"... the mitochondria, which you'll recall is the powerhouse of the cell."

I know people in tech like this...

People assume all of tech is like this...
