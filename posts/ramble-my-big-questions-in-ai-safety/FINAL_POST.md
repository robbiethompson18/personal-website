## Alignment

Is technnical alignment as theoretically difficult as Yudkowsky claims it is?

How powerful of AI can we create while putting off the 'big questions'? What are the big questions?

- If we are utilitarian
- If AI should engage in agency morality vs care morality
- How rigorously we need to define people's rights and who is a moral subject

Is it even desirable to live in a world where humans are not the most powerful being? People seem to
really like God. These seems like a very powerful and desirable meme. Maybe AI can fill that role?

## Control

What is the end goal of the control? Do we really expect to catch models _red_ handed, where they
admit 'ah yeah I was trying to take over the world'? Can you make a model organism for this? How do
you trade off 'it's really trying' with 'holy shit this thing is hilariously dangerous'? Are these
models organisms even net-good? What if we just teach Sama how to make an AI with secret allegiances
that he can hide?

What updates can we ever make? If Astra showing up as aligned on benchmarks is bad, what is good?
