## Unaffiliated

It always surprises me how rarely people are willing to affiliate with any group:

- At the last [ACX meetup](https://www.astralcodexten.com/p/meetups-everywhere-2026-times-and) I was
  the only person willing to call myself an EA in my six person conversation circle, even though
  almost everyone was on board with effective charities and worried about AI.
- I've dated people who were seeing multiple partners, wanted to continue seeing multiple partners,
  but refused to call themselves poly.
- Most people will hem and haw if you call them a liberal or conservative or Democrat or
  Republican.[^libertarian]
- I meet a lot more Rationalist-adjacent people than I do Rationalists.

I hate this pattern.

## Don't Hedge

Unwillingness to identify with a community is a hedge. People don't want to tie their reputations to
others'. They would rather communicate no information than communicate mostly true information. They
try not to commit to opinions.

I treat my persona like a binary search problem. Once I realize I'm wrong about something, I adjust
my beliefs and actions in a big way. I over-adjust less than half the time, which means I should be
updating even more.

## Committing to the Bit

I am a chameleon, and feel no need to be ashamed about this. Some examples:

In high school I was the captain and MVP of the football team. Our school didn't have enough chubby
kids, so they made me play on the line. I ate everything in sight for 18 months straight trying to
put on weight. Three months after playing my last down of football I was 40 pounds lighter.

I was in a frat in college. I _loved_ it. People talk about how being in the woods makes you feel
alive. Wolfing down 12 tacos and a gallon of beer in 20 minutes with 30 of your best friends while
50 drunk mongrels scream at you to go faster made me feel pretty darn alive.

During COVID I slept in a tent for four months straight on a ranch in Patagonia. I knew all 15 cows
and 20 horses by name.

For four years I was vegan. I cooked meals at the vegan co-op on campus. My frat friends thought
this was very weird.

I have done two ten-day silent meditation retreats.

The same outdoorsy vegan meditator from the previous three entries spent his first 18 months out of
college trading bonds for Jane Street. I regret not committing to this bit harder; I clung to my
stale hippy identity.

My friend Bailey and I wanted to found a company, so we quit our jobs to do YC, sent many thousands
of cold emails trying to get people to buy our software, and eventually quit when we realized we
couldn't sell water in hell.

I read ACX and I _am_ a Rationalist (not adjacent) and an EA (not adjacent). I donate to effective
charities and work on AI x-risk and am stoked to polygenically select the embryos which will become
my children.

My persona is full of contradictions. I am not bothered.
