## Object-level Opinions

### Pause is most important

- AI going poorly seems somewhat overdetermined at this point; we should almost certainly slow down
  ASAP. Risks that we aren't close to figuring out: loss of control, gradual disempowerment,
  schemers, concentration of power
- The blockers for a pause are political and technical.
- I would probably do the most good taking a more political role. I think politics/policy is the
  crux. Though I would be so much higher leverage in DC if I took a real AI role for six months
  first which makes METR sensible.
- The technical policy stuff is also very very important. I don't know how valuable I would be
  gathering talent here, but I think it's plausible.

### Hard to determine sign of any capabilities work, don't buy the differentiation thesis much

- For example: better at DFIR -> better at covering up tracks. Better at cyber, better at hacking

### Bio is important, but I have no background

QED.

### Cyber: angle I most buy is securing lab datacenters

- improving model capabilities: super dual-use, probably net negative
- I guess generic cybersecurity hardening of the world is important but not that existential; I
  doubt this will be the crux in whether the AIs win some civil war
- rogue deployments are very scary, want to prevent even Astra++ from doing this
- CCP stealing weights is very scary; this basically instantly burns our lead and massively
  intensifies a race (because we are so close to RSI)
- This seems very much like a 'get humans/labs collectively good at a specific defensive capability'
  matters, _not_ 'make models good at cyber'
