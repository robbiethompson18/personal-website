## Where Your Premises Were Off

- **"Malaria gone from South America."** The Americas had 536,700 cases in 2024. Only Paraguay,
  Argentina and Suriname are certified malaria-free.
- **"Eliminated the screwworm."** Eliminated regionally and held by a barrier in Panama that cost
  about $20M a year. The barrier failed; the first US case was June 3, 2026.
- **"Kept the Mississippi in Louisiana."** Both routes are in Louisiana. The Corps keeps it flowing
  past New Orleans instead of down the Atchafalaya.
- **"Those beetles."** Almost certainly the cane toad, introduced to Australia in 1935 to eat cane
  beetles.
- **"Canals in USA (Erie, Idaho)."** Idaho's canals are irrigation, not transport.
- **Aral Sea as a "fail".** The shrinkage was planned, not an accident.
- **"Increase global rainfall, this would probably be good."** The global total is not something you
  can raise without warming the planet, and it is the wrong target.

## Where the Files Disagree or the Numbers Are Soft

- **Crops under stratospheric sulfur.** File 04 cites a 2018 study of Pinatubo finding the dimming
  cancels the cooling benefit for maize. File 05 cites a 2021 modeling study finding yields up 10%
  under geoengineering. Unresolved.
- **Net lives saved by sulfur injection.** The 13-to-1 ratio in file 04 has a 90% interval that
  crosses zero.
- **New York's "3 million curb spaces".** File 10 argues it is probably a large overcount; the only
  block-level inventory found 1.28 million.
- **Golden Gate Park redwood heights.** No published figure found; 30-45 m in file 09 is the agent's
  estimate.
- **Waterfall pumping bill.** The $140k a year in file 08 assumes the falls run 12 hours a day.
- **Agent-constructed estimates.** The Sahara irrigation cost, the Bay fill cost and value per acre,
  the US swimmable-rivers total, the per-tree climbing cost, and the Great Salt Lake and Colorado
  River annual totals are all the agents' own arithmetic. Each file shows the inputs.
- **Secondary sourcing.** Several figures in file 05 come from secondary sources because the papers
  were paywalled; each is marked in the file.
